Organization

Who we are

From Waste to Worth is a student project organization created for CSC 375 at the University of Victoria. Our team is organized into client and vendor roles and works together to analyze a real operational problem, define system requirements, model a proposed solution, and communicate project progress through formal course deliverables. Our current work focuses on the analysis and design of a food-redistribution management system for the UVic campus context.

Client background

Who the project serves

The client represents the management teams of the UVSS and University Food Services operations. UVic campus food services generate edible surplus food with a short shelf life. Currently, coordination for donations and pickups relies heavily on informal communication such as emails, instant messages, and phone calls. Without a centralized system, usable food may not be redistributed in time and can end up in the landfill.

Project description

Executive summary

From Waste to Worth focuses on improving food redistribution around the UVic campus. Food services may have prepared food and other edible surplus that is still suitable for consumption, but the food can have a short remaining usable life. When coordination relies on emails, messages, and phone calls, it can be difficult to arrange donations and pickups quickly enough.

The proposed system provides a common workflow for recording surplus food, checking donation eligibility, coordinating approved recipients, organizing pickup and transportation, tracking donations, and supporting reporting. The initial scope covers selected food services, the redistribution program, and a limited number of redistribution partners around the UVic campus.

This course project covers the analysis and design of the proposed system. Development and deployment of a production system are outside the current scope.
